Eight weeks to draw from imagination

You cannot draw from imagination what your brain cannot construct mechanically. So this is not a course about talent — it is eight weeks of isolating one technical variable a day until construction becomes unconscious, and your conscious mind is free for composition and storytelling.

Giving away my entire drawing class
brokendraw · 43 min · the video this whole plan is built from. Each week also links to its own timestamp.
Medicine, not diet
Drills are medicine for a specific weakness. The project is the meal. Drills alone produce exercise technicians who can draw 250 perfect cubes and still freeze at a blank page.
The 50% rule
Day seven of every week belongs to joy, play and curiosity. Zero drills, zero self-critique. If all your drawing is rigorous evaluation, your brain learns to associate the sketchbook with failure.
Whole-arm pivot
Lock the wrist for long curves and perspective lines. Pivot from the shoulder and elbow. Cold hands draw from the wrist and produce timid, scratchy, hairy lines.

A day has three parts

A · Warm-up
10 min
Motor calibration. Activates the kinetic chain from core to shoulder so you commit clean single-pass strokes.
B · The drill
45–60 min
The medicine. Isolates one variable — convergence, minor axes, boolean cuts, fabric tension — with no pressure to make a finished artwork.
C · The project
30–45 min
The meal. Stress-tests the day's drill in a real creative problem, burning the mechanical skill into your visual library.
D · Day seven
All day
Play. Doodles, fan art, imagination sketches. Scheduled recovery, not a skipped day.

Three sources

Goldilocks zone
Too easy is boredom, too hard is burnout. If a drill breaks you, add scaffolding — a ruled horizon, a pencil instead of a fineliner — then take it away.

Method & kit

Every day is warm-up, drill, project, plus a rest day that is not optional. Roughly 10–12 hours a week, 90–120 minutes a day, six days on.

Three anchor rules

Medicine, not diet
Drills are medicine to fix a specific weakness, not a diet. The project is the meal. Drills without application produce exercise technicians who draw 250 perfect cubes and freeze at a blank page.
brokendraw, p. 4
The 50% rule
Exactly half of your artistic life belongs to joy, play and curiosity. Day seven of every week is fan art, doodles and rough ideas with zero drills and zero self-critique.
Drawabox, Lesson 0
Whole-arm pivot
Lock your wrist for long curves and perspective lines. Pivot from the shoulder and elbow. Drawing from the wrist produces timid, scratchy, hairy lines.
Peter Han, pp. 4–10
